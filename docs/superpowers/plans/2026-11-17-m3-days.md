# Days, Material 3 (Stage 2): Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild `/days` in Material 3 from the prototype. The work covers:
- single-date plans layered on the weekly days;
- admin-set closed days;
- removing "Working remote".

**Architecture:**
- Two new store collections, `planOverrides` and `closedDays`, implemented in both adapters.
- The working-day rules in `lib/leave.ts` and `lib/office-days.ts` stay pure. They take an optional `closed` set, which `lib/days.ts` and the routes load once per request.
- The page is a client component under `components/days/`, built from `components/m3` primitives and wrapped in `m3 m3-page`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4.6, React 19, TanStack Query, Vitest, Playwright.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-17-m3-days-design.md`. The prototype snapshot is in `docs/design/m3/`: `Prototype.dc.html` ("Days" and "Home" tabs), `DesktopPrototype.dc.html` and `M3AdminClosedDays.dc.html`. Where the spec and the snapshot disagree, the snapshot wins on look and the spec wins on behaviour.

## Review Focus

1. **A plan override on a date that later becomes closed, or gets approved leave.** The day must show as closed or leave, not planned, and must not count in `plannedCount`. Test in Task 2 (`daysView` precedence).
2. **Month boundaries.** Planning in the next two months must work; the third month ahead gives 409. The next-month view should show weekly-pattern days as planned. Test in Task 3.
3. **Leave across a closed date or a weekend.** The cost counts only open working days. Test in Task 1 (`leaveCost` with `closed`).
4. **Checking in on a closed day.** The day is refused with 409 "The office is closed that day", and the check-in card shows that message, not a generic error. Test in Task 3 and Task 6.
5. **Two quick taps on "Plan to come in".** The second tap must not flip the plan back. The sheet closes on the first success, and the button is disabled while pending. Test in Task 5's e2e (double click leaves the day planned).

### Task 3: Routes

**Files:**
- Create: `app/api/days/plan/route.ts`, `app/api/admin/closed-days/route.ts`
- Modify: `app/api/days/route.ts`, `lib/days.ts` (`markDay`), `app/api/away/route.ts` (leave cost and plan clearing), `lib/admin-view.ts`
- Test: `e2e/api.spec.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- `PATCH /api/days/plan` with body `{ date, planned: boolean }`.
  - Returns 200 with the `DaysView` for the date's month.
  - Returns 400 for a bad body.
  - Returns 409 `{ code: "not-plannable" }` unless the date is after today, no more than two calendar months ahead (`date.slice(0,7) <= shiftMonth(today, 2)`), working (closed included), and not on pending or approved leave.
  - It stores `null` when `planned` equals the pattern's value, so overrides only record differences.
- `POST /api/days`:
  - `mode` must be "office" or "none" (`"remote"` → 400 "Choose office or none").
  - Office on a closed date → 409 `{ code: "closed", error: "The office is closed that day" }`.
  - `MarkResult` gains `code: "closed"` with status 409.
- `/api/admin/closed-days` (admin only, 401 otherwise):
  - `GET ?from&to` returns `ClosedDayRow[]`.
  - `POST {date, reason}` requires a reason of 1–120 characters, rejects a past date with 409, and rejects a duplicate with 409 "Already closed".
  - `DELETE {date}` returns 404 if the date isn't closed.
- `POST /api/away`:
  - Cost and the non-working check use `closed`.
  - After saving a leave request, it calls `setPlanOverride(email, d, false)` for each pattern day in the range and removes true overrides.
- Admin overview: `counts` becomes `{ office, notIn, leave }`, where `notIn` covers remote and not marked. `TodayState` drops "remote", and remote rows map to null.

  - a member gets 401 on closed-days;
  - leave over a closed date costs one fewer day.
